# Shrinkage grid search — blending the model toward the market

- **Blend**: `p_w = w * model_prob + (1 - w) * market_fair`, applied inside the walk-forward at the point `size_bet` is called
- **Bands**: `walkforward` · **warm-up (no bets)**: 2020-21 · 1,900 matches priced
- **Staking**: unchanged production `pipeline.staking` — +5% EV floor, Quarter-Kelly ceiling, Eighth-Kelly at or above +20%, 3% per-bet cap
- **Calibration**: Brier score over every priced selection (population fixed across w). Lower is better.
- **Yield**: production staking on the bets each w selects (population MOVES with w — read `bets` alongside).

`w = 1.00` is the production model as it stands; `w = 0.00` is the de-vigged market with no model input. Rows with fewer than 100 bets are flagged `thin_sample` — a yield on that few bets is not a measurement.
