### Hull City — Sergej Jakirović

**CV:** Bosnian (49), appointed Hull head coach summer 2025, won promotion via play-offs in his first season — his first English management job after a 20-year, five-country playing career (defensive midfielder) and management spells at Sesvete, Gorica, Maribor, Zrinjski Mostar (2021/22 Bosnian title), Rijeka, Dinamo Zagreb (league/cup double), and Kayserispor in Turkey. Career record: 1.84 goals/game, 58.9% win ratio across 292 matches in four countries. Chairman Acun Ilıcalı specifically wanted the attacking style he'd seen from Jakirović's Kayserispor side; this is Hull's fourth head coach in just over a year under Ilıcalı's ownership.

**Philosophy:** Explicitly attacking and adaptive — "my style, my football philosophy, is adapt as soon as possible to the team to develop them." Wants fast transitions, high press, numbers in the box, deliberately not a possession-for-possession's-sake side. Not dogmatic about system: changed formation mid-week during the Championship season based on specific opposition threats (e.g. switched to a back five to nullify Middlesbrough).

**Formation & shape:** Base is 4-3-3, but used a back three with wing-backs for the play-off semi-final second leg at Millwall and the final, and has shown willingness to flip to 3-4-2-1 or a back five reactively within a single week. Expect Premier League Hull to open in a 4-3-3/4-2-3-1 hybrid with the back-three variant kept in the locker for specific matchups.

**Playing Style Profile**

| **Tempo** | High | Fast transitional emphasis both directions |

| **Defensive line** | Reactive | Adjusts height/shape week to week based on opposition (see Middlesbrough/Bristol City examples) |

### Coventry City — Frank Lampard

**CV:** Appointed November 2024 after Coventry sacked club favourite Mark Robins with the side 17th in the Championship. **Philosophy:** Direct, high-energy, relentless final-third focus — "a team built in his own image." Not dogmatic on shape; converts fluidly between back three and back four in build-up depending on the game state. Recruitment profile under him: forwards who attack the box in numbers, midfielders who arrive late into it.

**Formation & shape:** Predominantly 4-3-3/4-2-3-1 at Coventry, with a back-three spell used early in his tenure. Matt Grimes drops from the double pivot to form a back three on the ball, freeing both full-backs to sit high and wide — a clear structural link to Arsenal-style buildup patterns, unsurprising given his time under similar principles as a player. Genuine tactical flexibility, not a fixed system.

**Playing Style Profile**
