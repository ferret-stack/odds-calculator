## Chelsea — Liam Rosenior

### Manager Profile

**Liam Rosenior** (appointed January 6, 2026, contract until 2032). Age 41. Former defender who played for Bristol City, Fulham, Reading, Hull City, and Brighton. Coaching career: Brighton U23 assistant (2018), Derby County assistant under Wayne Rooney, Hull City head coach (2023-24), Strasbourg head coach (2024-26). Philosophy centers on combining "the beautiful with the practical" - possession football with purposeful vertical transitions. Became fifth permanent BlueCo manager at Chelsea.

### Formation & Shape

- **Primary:** 4-2-3-1, continuing Maresca's system initially
- **Attacking shape:** 3-2-2-3 in possession. Both full-backs can invert centrally alongside Caicedo
- **Alternative:** 3-4-2-1 used vs Napoli - can switch between back 3 and 4
- **Defensive shape:** Can form back 5 when opponents deploy 5 attackers - winger may drop to create 5-2-3
- **Key tactical differences from Maresca:** More vertical passing, wingers hold width more, full-backs invert together and play narrower

### Playing Style Profile

|Aspect|Rating|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|**Pressing**|High|Aggressive counter-pressing after losing ball. Man-to-man marking. PPDA 11.8 at Strasbourg (similar to Chelsea's 10.0 under Maresca)|
|**Build-up**|Patient/Vertical|Short passes from back to invite press, then vertical piercing passes. Through-balls prioritized. GK heavily involved|
|**Tempo**|Variable|Patient in early build-up, suddenly switches to high tempo transitions. Fewer fast breaks than Maresca's Chelsea|
|**Width**|Wingers wide|Wingers hug touchline for 1v1s. Full-backs invert inside, play narrower than under Maresca|
|**Defensive line**|Medium-high|Sits deeper than elite pressing teams. Mid-block with man-to-man elements|
|**Possession**|55-60%|Ranks high for passes and touches but doesn't dominate possession percentage|

### Tactical Identity

**Build-up Philosophy:** Borrows from Roberto De Zerbi. "Provocation" is key - goalkicks from 6-yard box, soles on ball, passes into pressure to bait opposition press. When opponent bites, plays through them quickly. GK acts as 11th outfield player.

**Full-back Usage:** Both full-backs invert simultaneously, sitting either side of Caicedo. Creates narrow shape. Differs from Maresca who gave more freedom to overlap. James and Cucurella held much narrower vs Brentford than under Maresca.

**Winger Role:** Hold width, create 1v1 situations. With full-backs inverting inside, wingers have space to attack opposition full-backs in isolation.

**Counter-Pressing:** Three of four goals in first two home games came from high turnovers. Players follow man into midfield. Aggressive but not manic - selective pressing triggers.

### Strengths

- High turnovers leading to goals - counter-pressing effectiveness
- Youth development - fielded Ligue 1's first all-U23 French outfield lineup at Strasbourg
- Tactical flexibility - uses 8 different systems at Strasbourg
- Player-adaptive - adjusts shape to suit squad profiles, doesn't force players into blueprint
- Knows BlueCo system - already aligned with ownership model
- Quick start: Won 5 straight including Napoli (3-2) and West Ham (3-2)

### Weaknesses

- Set-piece vulnerability - conceded from set-piece vs Arsenal in EFL Cup
- Defensive instability - only 2 clean sheets under Rosenior, often concedes 2+
- Error-prone build-up - Strasbourg had joint-most errors leading to goals in Ligue 1
- Inexperienced at top level - no Premier League management experience
- Communication issues - has criticized referees and players publicly at times

### Key Players Under Rosenior

- **Andrey Santos:** Flourished under Rosenior at Strasbourg (11 goals, 4 assists). Likely to get more minutes
- **Liam Delap:** Worked with Rosenior at Hull. Rosenior said: "He is one of a kind and I love him"
- **Cole Palmer:** Key No.10. System designed around him
- **Moises Caicedo:** Anchor in double pivot. Protected by inverting full-backs
- **Alejandro Garnacho:** Getting regular starts on flanks

## Manchester United — Michael Carrick (Interim)

### Manager Profile

**Michael Carrick** (appointed January 13, 2026, interim until end of season). Age 44. Club legend - 464 appearances, 5 Premier League titles, Champions League winner. Coaching career: Man United assistant under Mourinho and Solskjaer, caretaker manager (2021, 3 games W2 D1), Middlesbrough head coach (Oct 2022 - June 2025). Philosophy: possession-based, control-oriented football with quick short passing and emphasis on playing through the middle. Favors 4-2-3-1 with fluid No.10.

### Formation & Shape

- **Primary:** 4-2-3-1 (112 of 124 Championship games at Middlesbrough)
- **Attacking shape:** 3-2-5 in possession. One full-back pushes high, other tucks inside
- **Alternative:** 4-4-2 or 4-3-2-1 "Christmas Tree". Rarely changes formation mid-match
- **Defensive shape:** 4-4-1-1 or 4-4-2 low block. Shows ball wide, protects central areas
- **Key change from Amorim:** Back four instead of back three. Players in natural positions. More defensively solid

### Playing Style Profile

|Aspect|Rating|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|**Pressing**|Selective|Not coordinated high press. Players win individual battles. Striker and No.10 press passing lanes|
|**Build-up**|Short passing|Dominated central spaces at Boro. Double pivot connects with CBs via bounce passes to manipulate press|
|**Tempo**|Controlled|Lots of passes, deliberately patient. Ranked top for passes at Boro. Dictates tempo like Carrick the player|
|**Width**|Asymmetric|Left-back attacks high and wide. Right-back more conservative, tucks in|
|**Defensive line**|Mid-to-low block|Patient in defense. Shows ball to flanks. Protects central channels|
|**Possession**|55%+|Possession-dominant at Boro. Grinds down opposition through keeping ball|

### Tactical Identity

**The No.10 Role:** Central to Carrick's system. At Boro, transformed striker Chuba Akpom into dynamic No.10 - he scored 29 goals. Expects the No.10 to make runs into the box from deep. Fernandes or Cunha likely to thrive here.

**Full-back Asymmetry:** Left-back is main attacking outlet - pushes high and wide to deliver crosses. Right-back stays conservative, tucks inside as hybrid CB/FB. At Boro, Ryan Giles got 11 assists from LB in one season.

**Central Dominance:** Double pivot plays lots of short bounce passes to manipulate opposition press. When defender is drawn forward, plays through the gap. Passing triangles everywhere.

**Counter-Attacking:** While possession-based, can unleash devastating counters. Derby goal vs City showed Fernandes driving through middle, slipping in Mbeumo. Long diagonal balls also encouraged - Martinez key passer.

### Early Results at Man United

|Match|Result|Notes|
|---|---|---|

|vs Fulham (Feb 1, H)|Won 3-2|Sesko stoppage-time winner. Three consecutive wins - matched Amorim's best run in one week|

### Strengths

- Immediate defensive solidity - moved to back four, players in natural positions
- Counter-attacking threat - devastating vs City and Arsenal
- Knows the club - 464 appearances, understands what United fans want
- Develops attacking talent - Akpom, Latte Lath, Morgan Rogers all thrived under him
- Raises morale - Martinez said derby was "best Old Trafford atmosphere" in his time
- Long diagonal passing encouraged - suits Martinez and Shaw

### Weaknesses / Caveats

- Never got Middlesbrough promoted despite three seasons
- Tactically inflexible - rarely changes shape mid-match
- Double pivot can be exposed - Hackney/Howson lacked defensive nous at Boro
- Interim status - only until end of season, may limit long-term planning
- Small sample size - only 3 PL games so far
